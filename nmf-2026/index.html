A strong night, in spite of the weather.

About 2,500 people came out to Lakeline Park for NightMarket.fun 2026. That is a strong turnout for an outdoor event with a heavily walk-up audience and a cold, rainy forecast all week. That forecast held: the rain started around 8:30 pm while guests were still arriving, and people stayed through it until we closed early around 9:30 pm.

Many returning guests told us it felt bigger than last year. This recap covers what H-E-B's presenting partnership delivered: the in-app shopper poll, the billboards, the on-site presence, and the audience H-E-B reached.
